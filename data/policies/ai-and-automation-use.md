# Purpose
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE POLICY written for this demo.

# Approved uses of AI tools
Client information may only be entered into AI tools that the firm has approved and contracted. Pasting client data into a
public chatbot or any unapproved AI service is prohibited.

# Human review of automated changes
Automation may prepare proposed changes to client records, but a person must review and approve each change before it
is applied. The reviewer must be different from whoever submitted the item. Exceptions are investigated, never
approved as-is.

# Logging and audit
Every automated action and every human decision is recorded in the audit log. Staff must not alter or delete audit
entries.

# Purpose
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE POLICY written for this demo. It is not Org's policy and not legal or compliance advice.

# Changing a client's email address
An email address on a client record may only be changed after the client's identity and intent are verified. A signed
form alone is not sufficient. Staff must confirm the change by calling the client on the phone number already on file,
or by a confirmation inside the secure client portal.

Record who verified the request, the date, and the method. If the new email differs from the one on file, the
reviewer must note that the verification was completed before approving the update.

# Changing a mailing address
A mailing address change requires a signed request. A confirmation letter is sent to the OLD address and the new
address. For accounts holding assets, advisors must also be notified of the change.

# Changing account ownership or beneficiaries
Ownership and beneficiary changes are never handled by automation. They require original legal documents and review by
a licensed advisor and the operations lead.

# Purpose
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE POLICY written for this demo. Restricted to the compliance team, so searches by other audiences must never return it.

# Reporting suspected fraud
Suspected fraud or suspicious account activity must be escalated to the Chief Compliance Officer within 24 hours. Do not
discuss the matter with the client. Do not tip off the person involved. Preserve all related documents.

# Regulatory inquiries
All regulator contact is routed through the Chief Compliance Officer. Staff must not respond directly.

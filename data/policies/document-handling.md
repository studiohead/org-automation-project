# Purpose
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE POLICY written for this demo. Retention periods below are invented for illustration.

# Handling signed documents
Completed, signed documents are stored in the designated SharePoint library for the client. Staff must not keep copies
on personal devices, personal email, or removable media.

# Retention of signed documents
Signed client documents are kept for seven years after the account is closed (example figure), then securely destroyed
unless a legal hold applies. Requests to delete earlier go to the compliance team.

# Sharing documents with outside parties
Client documents may be shared with an outside party, such as an attorney or accountant, only with the client's written
authorization. Use an encrypted channel or the secure client portal. Never send client documents as ordinary email
attachments.
